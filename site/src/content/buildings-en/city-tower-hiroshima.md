## The Chūgoku Region's Tallest Building

City Tower Hiroshima was completed on 31 August 2016 in Matsubara-cho, Minami Ward, Hiroshima City. The residential tower rises 197.5 metres over 52 floors, making it the tallest building in the Chūgoku region. The project was developed by Sumitomo Realty & Development and designed by R.I.A., the Tokyo-based architectural firm.

The complex pairs the residential West Tower with a lower-rise East Tower that operates under the BIG FRONT Hiroshima brand. That commercial wing houses a large electronics retailer, national-scale conference facilities and other public functions, creating a mixed-use gateway on the north side of Hiroshima Station. Direct connectivity to the station makes the complex a natural first impression of the city for travellers arriving by Shinkansen.

## Rebuilding the North Side of Hiroshima Station

The north exit area of Hiroshima Station had long lagged behind the development activity concentrated on the south side. City Tower Hiroshima was the centrepiece of an effort to rebalance that equation, linking the new tower to upgraded pedestrian infrastructure and retail development that gradually filled in around it after 2016.

From the upper floors, the tower commands views of the Ōta River delta that spreads across central Hiroshima, the islands of the Seto Inland Sea on clear days, and the distinctive silhouette of the city that has rebuilt itself with such deliberate care over the postwar decades.

## Summary

Completed in 2016, 52 storeys, 197.5 metres. City Tower Hiroshima set a new height record for the Chūgoku region and repositioned Hiroshima Station's north exit as a fully realised city gateway. For anyone visiting Hiroshima, the tower is a worthwhile landmark to walk around — and the view from the upper floors adds a rare aerial perspective to the city's already compelling story.
