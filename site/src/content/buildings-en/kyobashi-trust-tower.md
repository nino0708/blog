## A Mixed-Use Tower in the Heart of Kyobashi

Kyobashi Trust Tower completed in February 2014 in Kyobashi, Chuo Ward, a district midway between Ginza and Nihonbashi. The building rises 21 stories above ground with three basement levels, reaching 107.9 m and offering approximately 52,470 sqm of total floor area. Mori Trust served as developer, Yasui Architects & Engineers and Toda Corporation jointly handled design, and Toda Corporation carried out construction.

The building's most notable feature is its mixed program: the lower floors (1–4F) accommodate Courtyard by Marriott Tokyo Station, while the upper floors serve as office space. This combination suits the location's strong demand from business travelers given its proximity to Tokyo Station.

## Kyobashi's Transformation

Since the 2000s, Kyobashi has seen multiple large office and mixed-use buildings completed, gradually shifting from its traditional commercial character toward a denser urban office district. Kyobashi Trust Tower is part of that transformation, delivering a substantial block of office and hotel floor area to a historically significant address just minutes from Tokyo Station.

Mori Trust has developed several prominent urban properties across Tokyo, and this tower reflects the company's strategy of pairing office space with hospitality use in high-demand central locations.
