## A New Civic Tower for Yokohama

Yokohama City Hall rises 155.4 metres across 32 above-ground floors in the Kanai district of Naka Ward, Yokohama. Completed in 2020, it was designed by Fumihiko Maki, the Pritzker Prize-winning architect also responsible for Makuhari Messe and Tokyo's Spiral building. The complex pairs a slender high-rise office tower with a lower council chamber wing, creating a civic complex that accommodates both administrative functions and spaces open to the public — including a citizens' hall and ground-floor retail.

Yokohama is Japan's most populous city after Tokyo, with a population of over 3.7 million. Its previous city hall, built in the 1960s, had become overcrowded and outdated. The new hall was conceived to give the city's administration a modern, efficient home while also catalysing the revitalisation of the Kanai-Kankoku district, a historically significant but ageing commercial area. Maki's design keeps the tower slender to avoid dominating the surrounding urban fabric or blocking views toward Minato Mirai 21, and connects to the street through a covered arcade at ground level.

## Kanai Reborn

The move to the new city hall in 2020 was a milestone not just for city government but for urban planning in Yokohama. The vacated old city hall site became available for redevelopment, injecting new energy into a district that had struggled to compete with the glamour of Minato Mirai. Seen from the waterfront or from Odori Park, the 155.4-metre tower creates a clear counterpoint to the high-rises of Minato Mirai while remaining respectful of Kanai's lower-scale heritage streetscapes.

## Summary

Yokohama City Hall was completed in 2020 at 32 storeys and 155.4 m, designed by Fumihiko Maki. The tower's clean silhouette over the Kanai district marks a new chapter for one of Japan's most historically layered urban areas. Walk the old merchant streets around Basha-michi, cross to the new city hall plaza, and feel the centuries of Yokohama's port city story compressed into a single neighbourhood.
