## Tamurach Redeveloped

In Nishi-Shimbashi 1-chome, Minato City — in the low-rise Tamurach district between Shimbashi and Hibiya — Hibiya Fort Tower opened in July 2021. The building stands approximately 138 metres tall across 27 storeys above ground and two below. Nikken Sekkei provided the concept design; the design–build joint venture of Shimizu Corporation and Nikken Sekkei handled detailed design, with Shimizu Corporation as general contractor. The building reached completion on 30 June 2021 and opened on 1 July.

Tamurach had long been a neighbourhood of prewar office buildings and small mixed-use blocks. The urban redevelopment project that produced Hibiya Fort Tower cleared part of that fabric and replaced it with a high-rise, high-specification office building. The glass and metal-panel facade reads as modern without being aggressive, and the ground level incorporates public open space and greenery to maintain a permeable pedestrian environment.

## Between Hibiya and Toranomon

Hibiya Fort Tower sits at the crossroads of two of Tokyo's most active redevelopment corridors. Hibiya Park, the Imperial Hotel and the Ginza district lie to the east; the Toranomon Hills series of towers — which have reshaped the neighbourhood over the past decade — lies to the west. Several Tokyo Metro and Toei Subway stations are within walking distance, including Uchisaiwaicho, Kasumigaseki and Shimbashi, giving the building strong transit access.

The lower floors contain retail and service uses that contribute to the daily rhythms of the surrounding streets. As part of the broader Toranomon–Hibiya arc of recent development, the building represents the incremental densification of a central Tokyo quarter that retains traces of its earlier, finer-grained character.
