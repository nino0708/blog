## A Redevelopment Landmark East of Osaki Station

Osaki Bright Tower completed in April 2015 in Kitashinagawa, Shinagawa Ward, just four minutes on foot from Osaki Station's east exit. The building stands 31 stories above ground with two basement levels, reaching 142.9 m and offering roughly 92,000 sqm of total floor area. Nikken Sekkei handled the design, and Taisei Corporation carried out construction for the Kitashinagawa 5-chome Block 1 Urban Redevelopment Association.

Before the project, this block was a mix of smaller buildings. The redevelopment association assembled the site and delivered a large-scale office floor that helped consolidate the Osaki East area as a business district.

## Osaki's Evolving Office Cluster

Osaki Station has been the focus of successive redevelopments since the late 1990s, including Gate City Osaki (1999), ThinkPark Tower (2004), and Osaki Bright Core (2004). Osaki Bright Tower is among the most recent large additions to this cluster, and at 142.9 m it adds another point to the Osaki skyline.

The station connects directly to Yamanote Line and Rinkai Line services, making the surrounding office district attractive to a wide range of tenants.

## Summary

Osaki Bright Tower, completed in 2015, is a 31-story, 142.9 m office building with approximately 92,000 sqm of floor space. Born from a district urban redevelopment initiative, it stands as one of the defining office towers east of Osaki Station in Shinagawa Ward.
