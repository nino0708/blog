## Tohoku's Tallest Tower at the Sendai Station Forecourt

At 1-3-1 Chuo, Aoba Ward, Sendai City, AER rises 31 floors above ground and 4 below, reaching a height of 145.5 meters directly beside the west exit of Sendai Station. Completed in 1998 and designed by Nippon Sekkei, it was the centerpiece of the Sendai Station West Exit North District urban redevelopment project, and for over a decade it stood as the tallest building in Sendai City.

The building's name derives from the initials of Aoba (the central ward), Economy, and Renaissance — a name that reflects the regenerative ambitions the project carried. The lower floors house retail and commercial space (reorganized since opening), while mid-level floors are occupied by offices and upper technical floors accommodate communications infrastructure. On the 31st floor, a free observation lounge offers panoramic views of Sendai's city grid and the distant Ou Mountains to the west.

## The Role of a Station Forecourt Tower

Sendai is the largest city in the Tohoku region, and its station forecourt has historically been where the city makes its strongest metropolitan statement. AER's opening in 1998 advanced that tradition: a 145.5-meter tower at the station's north side gave the Sendai approach a new vertical dimension, signaling the city's evolution from regional center to economic hub.

AER's underground floors connect to Sendai City Subway lines, letting commuters move through the building without stepping outdoors. This integration of transport, office, and retail in a single station-adjacent tower has become a template repeated across Japan, but AER was among the earlier instances executed at significant height in a regional capital. The [Sendai Trust Tower](/buildings/sendai-trust-tower/) (completed 2012) eventually surpassed its height, but AER remains the dominant presence at the station's northern edge.

## Summary

AER Sendai was completed in 1998 with 31 floors above ground, 4 below, and a height of 145.5 meters. Designed by Nippon Sekkei as part of the Sendai Station West Exit North District redevelopment, it served as Sendai's tallest building for over a decade. The free 31st-floor observation lounge makes it an easy first stop for Shinkansen arrivals: ride straight up from the station concourse, take in the layout of the city from above, and then step out into Sendai's streets.
