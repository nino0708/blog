## Kyushu's Largest Arena on the Hakata Waterfront

Marine Messe Fukuoka sits on the reclaimed Okihama waterfront of Hakata Ward, within easy reach of central Fukuoka. Hall A, which opened in 1995, is a basement-two, four-storey structure whose main arena accommodates up to 15,000 people — one of the largest indoor venues in Kyushu. The flat-floor hall can be reconfigured for trade shows, international conventions, concerts, and sporting events, giving it a programming calendar that is rarely idle.

The Okihama waterfront was developed from the 1990s as part of Fukuoka's push to build a convention and hospitality zone adjacent to the port. Sun Palace, hotels, and marine-related facilities cluster in the same area, while Hakata Station and the Tenjin commercial district lie a short ride away by bus or taxi. During large events the economic ripple felt across the city is substantial, partly because Fukuoka — the largest city in Kyushu — has historically had fewer large-scale indoor venues than the Tokyo or Osaka metro areas.

## Hall B and the Expansion of Exhibition Space

In 2021, Hall B opened alongside the original building, adding a significant amount of additional exhibition area. The two halls can operate independently or be used together for the largest international trade fairs and exhibitions, raising Marine Messe's capacity to compete for the MICE events — meetings, incentives, conventions, and exhibitions — that cities across East Asia now actively recruit.

Fukuoka's self-positioning as a startup and innovation hub has drawn a growing number of technology conferences and pitch events, adding a new strand to the venue's programming mix alongside more established fixtures like popular-music concerts and regional industrial shows.

## Summary

Marine Messe Fukuoka opened Hall A in 1995 (up to 15,000 capacity, four storeys above and two below ground) and expanded with Hall B in 2021 to create one of Kyushu's most versatile event venues. Its location on the Hakata Port waterfront keeps it connected to the city while hosting events from pop concerts to international conventions.
