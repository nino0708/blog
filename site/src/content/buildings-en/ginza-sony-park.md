## A Park in the Middle of Ginza

Ginza Sony Park stands at 5-chome in Ginza, one of Tokyo's most iconic shopping streets, on the very ground where the original Sony Building rose in 1966. That earlier building was demolished after more than half a century of service, and from 2018 to 2021 the cleared site briefly operated as an experimental open-air park — a temporary installation that attracted attention by treating valuable real estate as genuine public space. The experience informed the design of the permanent building that followed.

Construction of the new structure began in October 2022, and the building was completed in August 2024, with the official opening following in January 2025. The completed building has five floors above ground and four below, stands 34 meters tall, and has a total floor area of 4,390 square meters. General construction was handled by Takenaka Corporation. The design concept, credited to the Ginza Sony Park Project team, centers on the idea of a "park" — an open, permeable space rather than a conventional commercial box.

## Experience Over Commerce

The building's glass facade and the vertical voids connecting the floors above and below street level are intended to dissolve the boundary between the building interior and the streets outside. Each floor hosts immersive exhibitions, food and drink spaces, and multimedia environments where Sony's technology, music, film, and entertainment businesses converge. The emphasis is on engagement rather than sales: visitors come to experience, not primarily to buy.

This positioning reflects a broader shift in how flagship brand spaces function in the digital era, when physical retail must justify itself through experiences that cannot be replicated online. In Ginza, where luxury brands compete for square footage along the main boulevard, Sony has chosen to offer something different — a small, open building that feels less like a store and more like a cultural venue.

## Summary

Ginza Sony Park, completed in 2024 and opened in January 2025, is Sony's brand hub in central Ginza. At five floors above ground and four below, with a height of 34 meters and a total floor area of 4,390 square meters, it is compact by Ginza standards but unmistakable in character. The "park" concept gives the city a rare pocket of openness amid the dense commercial streetscape, and a stop here fits naturally into any Ginza walk.
