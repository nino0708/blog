## A Tower on the Edge of the Outer Garden

Park Court Jingumae Kitasando The Tower stands at 4-6-1 Sendagaya, Shibuya City, Tokyo. Completed in June 2023, the building rises 27 stories with one basement floor and contains a total of 472 residential units. It was developed by Mitsui Fudosan Residential and constructed by a joint venture of Shimizu Corporation and Fujita Corporation.

Sendagaya is a quiet district between the Meiji Jingu Gaien — Tokyo's leafy outer garden, famous for its ginkgo-lined promenade — and the new National Stadium, rebuilt for the 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games. Despite its relatively tranquil character, the neighborhood is well connected: Sendagaya Station on the JR Chuo-Sobu Line and Kokuritsu-Kyogijo Station on the Toei Oedo Line are both within walking distance, giving residents easy access to Shinjuku, Shibuya, and other central hubs.

## The Park Court Series in a Rare Location

Park Court is Mitsui Fudosan Residential's high-end tower condominium brand, with buildings across central Tokyo and other major cities. The series tends to seek out locations that combine strong transit access with proximity to greenery or water. The Kitasando project — its name referencing the northern approach road to Meiji Shrine — is a strong example: the building sits close to the shrine's outer garden, offering upper-floor views across tree canopy in one of Tokyo's more forest-like central districts.

The tower's 27-story profile makes it one of the taller residential structures in the Sendagaya area. Its completion in 2023 introduced a prominent vertical form into a neighborhood previously defined by low-rise streets, the stadium's vast roof, and the treetops of the outer garden.

## Summary

Park Court Jingumae Kitasando The Tower is a 27-story, 472-unit condominium tower that completed in June 2023 in Sendagaya, Shibuya City. Developed by Mitsui Fudosan Residential and built by Shimizu and Fujita corporations, it occupies a position adjacent to the Jingu Gaien and the National Stadium. The area around the tower — combining parkland, shrine precincts, and a recently rebuilt international-caliber stadium — makes for an unusually verdant walk through the heart of Tokyo.
