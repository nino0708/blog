## A 43-Story Tower on the Akasaka Hillside

Park Court Akasaka The Tower rises 43 stories — 157 meters — above a hillside site in Akasaka 4-chome, Minato City. Completed in 2009, it was developed by Mitsui Fudosan Residential under its premium "Park Court" condominium brand. The building contains 518 units across its 43 floors above grade and two basement levels.

The location in Akasaka 4-chome sits in a topographically interesting part of Minato City: a sloping neighborhood that lies between the government quarter of Nagatacho — home to the National Diet Building and the Prime Minister's residence — and the livelier streets of Akasaka known for restaurants, hotels, and entertainment. Minato City's hilly terrain, formed by the old low-lying rivers and elevated plateaus that shaped Edo, gives this site a natural elevation advantage. Upper floors reportedly offer open views across central Tokyo in multiple directions.

The Park Court brand also appears in a separate Akasaka tower — Park Court Akasaka Hinokicho The Tower, which stands in Akasaka 9-chome adjacent to Hinokicho Park. Despite sharing a brand name, the two buildings occupy distinctly different parts of the Akasaka area, each with its own surrounding character.

## Akasaka's Residential Depth

Akasaka has long served as one of Minato City's most layered neighborhoods — embassy district, broadcasting hub (TBS is headquartered nearby), dining and nightlife corridor, and now an increasingly dense residential zone. The area around the former Akasaka Prince Hotel was redeveloped as Tokyo Garden Terrace Kioicho, while the Akasaka Sacas precinct brought a new commercial and entertainment complex near TBS.

Park Court Akasaka The Tower was completed in 2009, a challenging moment for the Tokyo real estate market in the aftermath of the global financial crisis. Yet this 43-story tower proceeded to completion, adding 518 homes to the Akasaka hillside. Walking the slopes of Akasaka 4-chome — up from the subway station, past small restaurants and office buildings — puts the tower's hilltop position into physical perspective.

## Summary

Park Court Akasaka The Tower was completed in 2009 as a 43-story, approximately 157-meter condominium tower in Akasaka 4-chome, Minato City. Developed by Mitsui Fudosan Residential with 518 units, it occupies a hillside address between Nagatacho and the Akasaka entertainment district. Together with its sister building Park Court Akasaka Hinokicho The Tower, it illustrates the range of residential options that have emerged across the expansive Akasaka neighborhood over recent decades.
