## White Wings Over an Industrial Shore

Tsurumi Tsubasa Bridge spans the port area of Tsurumi Ward, Yokohama, in the heart of the Keihin Industrial Zone. Opened in 1994, it carries National Route 357 — the Tokyo Bay Waterfront Road — across the busy commercial harbor. Its tall white towers send diagonal cables fanning out to support the deck in a form that reads as unexpectedly elegant against the smokestacks, oil tanks, and factory buildings that define this stretch of Japan's most concentrated heavy-industry corridor. Where most of the surrounding infrastructure is horizontal, massive, and functional, the bridge introduces clean verticals and a geometry of tension that catches the eye from a considerable distance. From the roadway, the view opens across the Tsurumi port basin toward the broader expanse of Tokyo Bay, with the working waterfront spread below in a panorama available nowhere else in this part of Yokohama.

## A Crossing Built for Industry

The Keihin Industrial Zone has anchored Japan's heavy industry since the Meiji era, stretching from Tokyo's waterfront through Kawasaki and into Yokohama with oil refineries, steelworks, and chemical plants occupying the coastal strip. By the 1990s, the road network connecting these facilities had become a priority, and the expansion of the Tokyo Bay Waterfront Road brought several major bridge projects to the area. Tsurumi Tsubasa Bridge was one of them — designed to leap the harbor opening while keeping the shipping lane clear and anchoring the route with a structure that could serve as a navigational landmark for the entire bay corridor. The cable-stayed form, favored for its ability to cover long spans without central piers obstructing navigation below, made it well suited to this demanding waterfront context. Today it carries the continuous flow of freight vehicles and commuters that keep the industrial zone functioning, and it has become one of the most recognizable structures along the Route 357 bayshore.

## Summary

Completed in 1994, Tsurumi Tsubasa Bridge is a cable-stayed bridge on National Route 357 that crosses the port of Tsurumi, Yokohama, linking the segments of the Tokyo Bay Waterfront Road across the Keihin Industrial Zone. The white towers rising above the working harbor offer a compelling illustration of how bridge engineering can create landmark architecture within a purely industrial landscape.
