## Power Company Headquarters on the Nakanoshima Island

Nakanoshima, Kita Ward, Osaka — a narrow island cradled between the Dojima and Tosabori rivers. In December 2004, a 41-story, 195-metre skyscraper completed here: the Kanden Building. Developed by Kanden Real Estate Development, a subsidiary of Kansai Electric Power, and designed by Nikken Sekkei and Newjec, the building rises to a total height of approximately 195.45 metres. It serves as the headquarters of the Kansai Electric Power Group, one of Japan's largest regional utilities.

Nakanoshima has long been Osaka's civic and financial core. The eastern end of the island holds City Hall and landmarks such as the Central Public Hall and the City Library — all built during the Meiji and Taisho eras. The western portion where the Kanden Building stands has been home to energy and financial industry headquarters since the early twentieth century. Completing this tower in 2004 represented a major reinvestment in that tradition, updating the company's base of operations to a high-rise suited to modern requirements.

## Architecture for an Infrastructure Owner

A utility's headquarters imposes demands that a typical commercial office building does not. Self-sufficient facilities, robust seismic performance, and business-continuity planning all feed directly into the building programme. The Kanden Building incorporates damping systems representative of the technology available at the time, paired with an energy-conscious facade. Newjec — also a Kansai Electric Power Group company — contributed structural and mechanical engineering, embedding in-house expertise throughout the design process.

The mid-2000s were an active period for large-scale redevelopment in Osaka. Plans for the Nakanoshima Festival Tower were advancing in nearby streets, and the Kitayardsuare (Grand Front Osaka) site was moving toward a decision. Seen in that context, the Kanden Building was an early mover that reshaped the western Nakanoshima horizon before the broader wave of redevelopment arrived.

## Summary

The Kanden Building, completed in 2004, is a 41-floor, 195-metre office tower that embodies the functional priorities of a major utility while adding a prominent new element to Nakanoshima's skyline.
