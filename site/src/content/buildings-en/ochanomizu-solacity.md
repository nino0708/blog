## An Office Tower on Ochanomizu's Clifftop

At 4-6 Kanda Surugadai, Chiyoda Ward, Tokyo, Ochanomizu Sola City opened in April 2013, directly connected to JR Ochanomizu Station and a short walk from Tokyo Metro's Shin-Ochanomizu Station. The building spans 23 floors above ground and 2 below, with a total floor area of approximately 102,000 square meters — one of the largest office buildings in Chiyoda Ward. Design and construction were handled by Taisei Corporation.

The office zone runs from the 6th to the 23rd floors, with each level offering approximately 3,000 square meters of leasable space — a mega-floor format that attracts large corporate tenants seeking contiguous workspace. The lower floors hold conference facilities, academic and educational institutions, and ground-level dining. The building's name, Sola City, reflects the site's commanding position on the clifftop above the Kanda River gorge.

## Ochanomizu's Double Life

Ochanomizu is one of the few districts in central Tokyo where the academic and the commercial have coexisted since the Meiji era. Medical schools, law schools, and music colleges cluster nearby, while the slopes below the station are lined with bookshops and instrument dealers serving students and professionals for generations. Into this setting, Sola City introduced a large, modern workplace seconds from the station gate.

The building's catchment spans several adjacent areas: [Akihabara UDX](/buildings/akihabara-udx/) to the east, university campuses nearby, and the Otemachi–Marunouchi business corridor to the south. This overlap has drawn a concentration of technology, healthcare, and professional-services firms. Since its 2013 opening, Sola City has helped accelerate a broader transformation of the Kanda Surugadai area, where older buildings have steadily given way to newer, larger-floor-plate alternatives.

## Summary

Ochanomizu Sola City was completed in 2013, with 23 floors, 2 basement floors, and a total floor area of approximately 102,000 square meters. Built by Taisei Corporation and directly connected to JR Ochanomizu Station, it is among the largest office complexes in Chiyoda Ward. To discover the site, arrive at Ochanomizu Station, walk through the building's ground-floor atrium, then descend toward the Kanda River to look back up at the cliff edge where the tower stands — a short walk that reveals the dramatic topography beneath this part of the city.
