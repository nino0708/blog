## A Glass Cathedral on the Hokkaido Plain

ES CON FIELD HOKKAIDO stands at the heart of HOKKAIDO BALLPARK F VILLAGE in Kitahiroshima City, completed on December 31, 2022, and welcoming its first NPB season in spring 2023. The home of the Hokkaido Nippon-Ham Fighters spans six floors above ground and two below, with a total floor area of 121,563 m². What sets it apart from every other ballpark in Japan is the combination of natural grass and a retractable roof—a first for the country. The glass walls reach a maximum height of 70 m, flooding the field with natural light on clear days while the sliding roof protects spectators from Hokkaido's demanding weather. To keep natural turf healthy through the northern winter, underfloor heating and a dedicated turf environment control system were integrated into the structure from the start.

## A Village Built Around a Ballpark

The design team—Obayashi Corporation and American sports architecture firm HKS—set out to create not a stadium surrounded by a town but a town built around a stadium. F VILLAGE delivers on that vision: restaurants, a hotel, a hot spring bath, childcare, and farm experiences all share the site with the field, drawing visitors on days when no game is scheduled. The seating bowl was engineered so that every section—from infield to outfield—feels close to the grass, with carefully calculated sightline angles throughout. The project cost has been reported at approximately ¥60 billion, making it one of the largest privately funded sports facilities in Japan and a reference point for ballpark redevelopment discussions across the country.

## Summary

Completed in 2022 and standing six stories tall over a floor area of 121,563 m², ES CON FIELD HOKKAIDO broke new ground as Japan's first natural-grass, retractable-roof ballpark. Beyond the game itself, F VILLAGE turns a visit into a full-day outing under the wide Hokkaido sky—well worth the trip to Kitahiroshima.
