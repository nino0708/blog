## A New Silhouette for a Four-Century-Old District

In Chuo Ward, Tokyo, where Nihonbashi has served as Japan's commercial and cartographic centre since the Edo period, a 26-story mixed-use tower completed in March 2019 added a sharp new line to the neighbourhood's historic roofscape. Nihonbashi Muromachi Mitsui Tower stands 140.5 metres tall and covers a gross floor area of approximately 166,000 square metres over 26 floors above ground and three below.

Developed by Mitsui Fudosan and designed by a team comprising Nihon Sekkei, Kajima Design, and Pelli Clarke Pelli Architects Japan, the building opened its commercial floors in September 2019 as COREDO Muromachi Terrace — the fourth instalment in the COREDO Muromachi series that Mitsui Fudosan has built across the Muromachi district since 2010. The lower levels include retail, dining, and a cinema, while the upper floors offer office space directly connected to Mitsukoshimae Station on the Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hanzomon lines.

## The Nihonbashi Revitalization Plan

The building is a central piece of the Nihonbashi Revitalization Plan, a long-term urban renewal effort that Mitsui Fudosan has led in collaboration with local landowners and the city of Tokyo. The plan aims to restore Nihonbashi — which spent decades overshadowed by the elevated expressway built for the 1964 Olympics — to its historical status as a premier address for commerce, culture, and urban life.

By filling in the final large vacant site in the Muromachi redevelopment zone, Nihonbashi Muromachi Mitsui Tower completes a continuous pedestrian network linking all four COREDO towers and connecting directly to the underground station concourse. The result is a weatherproof walking route that flows from Mitsukoshimae Station through retail and dining spaces, emerging into the street grid that leads toward Nihonbashi Bridge.

## Summary

Completed in 2019 at 26 floors and 140.5 metres, Nihonbashi Muromachi Mitsui Tower brings a world-class design collaboration to one of Tokyo's oldest commercial addresses. The COREDO Muromachi Terrace below it draws visitors from across the city; the offices above it signal Nihonbashi's continued relevance as a business hub. Stroll from the bridge along the Nihonbashi River, past the COREDO towers and their café terraces, and you will encounter the ongoing, cumulative project of restoring a great city's heart.
