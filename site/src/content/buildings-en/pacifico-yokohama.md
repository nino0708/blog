## Yokohama's Convention Hub on the Waterfront

Pacifico Yokohama — its official name is the Yokohama International Peace Conference Center — opened on 20 August 1991 on reclaimed land at the seaward end of the Minato Mirai 21 district. It was among the first large-scale facilities completed in the district, built to establish Yokohama as a destination for international conventions, academic conferences, and trade exhibitions.

The most recognisable feature of the complex is the Yokohama Grand Intercontinental Hotel: 31 above-ground floors, 2 basement floors, and a height of 140 metres. Its curved facade, designed to evoke a yacht's sail, has become one of the defining silhouettes of the Minato Mirai skyline since its opening in 1991. Alongside the hotel, the conference centre provides 33 meeting rooms of varying sizes, capable of accommodating events ranging from small breakout sessions to full plenary assemblies.

## A Complex That Grew Over Time

The National Convention Hall — the largest single venue in the complex — was completed in 1994, three years after the opening of the hotel and conference facilities. It seats 5,002 and is used for large plenary sessions at international conferences as well as concerts, ceremonies, and major trade events. Its curved roof is visible from outside the complex and gives the hall a distinct architectural presence alongside the sail-shaped hotel.

In April 2020, Pacifico Yokohama North opened as a new wing. It added over 6,000 square metres of exhibition space and a large meeting room, and it can be operated in combination with the original exhibition hall, which covers approximately 20,000 square metres. The opening coincided with the early months of the COVID-19 pandemic, but the expansion has since served the recovery in MICE demand.

Pacifico Yokohama predates the other major landmarks of Minato Mirai 21: Yokohama Landmark Tower opened in 1993 and Queen's Square Yokohama in 1997. As the first large-scale development in the district, the complex helped define what the reclaimed waterfront could become and drew subsequent investment to the area.

## Summary

Pacifico Yokohama opened in 1991 as an international convention complex anchored by the sail-shaped Yokohama Grand Intercontinental Hotel (31F, 140m). The 5,002-seat National Convention Hall followed in 1994, and Pacifico Yokohama North expanded the precinct further in 2020. Standing at the edge of Yokohama's reclaimed harbour, the complex has spent more than three decades drawing international events to the city.
