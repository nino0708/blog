## The Glass Tower at the West Edge of OBP

In Shiromi 1-chome, Chuo Ward, on the eastern edge of central Osaka — just beside Osaka Castle Park and along the south bank of the Okawa River — Crystal Tower completed in August 1990. Standing 37 storeys above ground and two below, with a total height of 157 metres, the tower was designed and co-developed by Takenaka Corporation together with Asahi Building. The total floor area reaches approximately 86,000 square metres.

The building's place in architectural history is secured by a single structural innovation: Crystal Tower was the first super-highrise building in Japan to incorporate a vibration-control structure. Where conventional earthquake-resistant tall buildings are designed to withstand seismic forces by stiffening, vibration-control systems work differently — dampers absorb and dissipate seismic energy, reducing the motion experienced inside the building. The completion of Crystal Tower in 1990 marked a turning point in how Japan's structural engineers and developers would approach tall-building earthquake safety from that point forward.

## OBP: Osaka's Privately Led Business District Experiment

Crystal Tower rises within Osaka Business Park, a purpose-built office district that began taking shape in the 1980s on land adjacent to the historic castle grounds. The concept — clustering private-sector high-rises in a planned precinct next to one of Japan's most famous landmarks — was ambitious for its time and positioned OBP as Osaka's answer to the government-led business district expansions happening in Tokyo.

Crystal Tower occupies the westernmost position in the OBP cluster, where it catches the eye of anyone crossing the Okawa from the north. Its all-glass facade, narrow floor plate, and tapering silhouette stand apart from neighbouring towers such as Twin 21, giving it a clarity of form that has aged well. Osaka City has since recognised it as part of the Ikita Kenchiku Museum Osaka Selection — an endorsement of its architectural quality more than three decades on.

Crystal Tower was completed in August 1990, rising 37 storeys to 157 metres in Osaka Business Park.
