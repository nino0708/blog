## Completing the Twin Towers of Umeda

Osaka Umeda Twin Towers South rose on the site of the former Osaka Shinnankaibu Building and the Shin-Hankyu Building — two landmark station structures that had defined Umeda's skyline for decades. Completed in 2022, the 38-storey, 188.9-metre tower brought the second half of the twin pair to life alongside [Osaka Umeda Twin Towers North](/buildings/osaka-umeda-twin-towers-north/) (completed 2012). The project was developed by Hankyu Hanshin Real Estate and designed by Nihon Sekkei together with Takenaka Corporation.

The lower floors of the South Tower house Hanshin Department Store's Umeda flagship, carrying on a retail tradition that stretches back to the original station building. The tower sits directly above the Umeda / Osaka Station interchange, where Hankyu, Hanshin and municipal subway lines converge, giving it one of the most connected locations in the Kansai region.

## A Century of Station-District Renewal

The predecessor buildings — one dating to 1932, the other to 1963 — were integral to Umeda's identity as Osaka's commercial heart. Their replacement took well over a decade of planning and phased construction. The arrival of the South Tower in 2022 finally closed the gap in the skyline, creating a coherent paired facade alongside the North Tower and reinforcing Umeda's position as the premier office address in the Kansai region.

The completion also deepened the link between [Nakanoshima Festival Tower West](/buildings/nakanoshima-festival-tower-west/) and the surrounding cluster of Osaka high-rises that emerged from the 2010s redevelopment wave.

## Summary

Completed in 2022, 38 storeys, 188.9 metres. Osaka Umeda Twin Towers South brought the second pillar of Umeda's defining skyline pair to completion, embedding Hanshin Department Store's flagship retail within a world-class office tower. Standing at street level and looking up at both towers side by side is one of the most striking urban views in modern Osaka — worth a detour on any visit to the city.
