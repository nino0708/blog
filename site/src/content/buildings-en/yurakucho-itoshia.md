## Stepping into the Gap Left by Sogo

When Sogo Yurakucho closed in 2000 following the department store group's bankruptcy, it left a prominent gap at one of central Tokyo's busiest station exits. The site — directly facing the Ginza exit of Yurakucho Station — sat at the junction between the Ginza shopping district and the cultural and business cluster around Tokyo International Forum. A development consortium including Tokyo Tatemono, Toho and Tokyu Corporation took on the site, and the result, completed in 2007, was Yurakucho Itoshia.

The building rises 21 floors above ground and four below, with the lower seven floors given over to Itoshia Plaza, a retail complex of restaurants, clothing, cosmetics and services. The upper floors contain office space. Direct connections to the station concourse make arrival and departure seamless, while underground passages extend the building's reach to Tokyo International Forum to the north and the Yurakucho Mullion complex and Hibiya to the south.

## Yurakucho's Place Between Ginza and the Forum

Yurakucho occupies an unusual position in central Tokyo: close enough to Ginza to share its atmosphere, but distinct enough to have its own identity built around the Forum, the old cinema district and the covered shopping alleys under the elevated railway tracks. Itoshia sits at the threshold, drawing from Ginza's foot traffic while also serving the business travellers and tourists who move between the station and the Forum.

The name Itoshia is a play on the Japanese phrase "愛しい場所" (itoshii basho, "a place I love"), signalling an intention to make the building a destination people feel attached to rather than merely transact in. The building replaced the Sogo store on a like-for-like footprint but with a different logic: mixed-use office above retail, compact and directly transit-connected, rather than a single-use department store.

## Summary

Yurakucho Itoshia, completed in 2007, is a 21-floor mixed-use building at the Ginza exit of Yurakucho Station in central Tokyo. Built on the former Sogo Yurakucho site, its lower floors house the Itoshia Plaza retail complex and its upper floors contain office space. Direct links to the station and underground connections to Tokyo International Forum make it one of the more convenient bases for exploring the Ginza and Hibiya area.
