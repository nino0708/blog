## A Mixed-Use Tower on a Historic Bank Site

Kitanomaru Square completed in January 2006 in Kudankita, Chiyoda Ward, just two minutes' walk from Kudanshita Station. The building stands 26 stories above ground with two basement levels, reaching 111.85 m and offering approximately 59,130 sqm of total floor area. Mitsubishi Jisho Sekkei designed the building and Kajima Corporation handled construction for a special-purpose vehicle, Palaceview LLC, backed by Mitsubishi Estate, Tokyu Land, and Deutsche Bank Group.

The site once held the headquarters of Nippon Credit Bank, which failed in 1998. The redevelopment transformed the former banking campus into a vertical mixed-use tower: retail on the ground floor, 14 floors of offices above it, and 11 floors of condominiums at the top.

## Between Yasukuni Shrine and Kitanomaru Park

The location is exceptional by any standard. The Kitanomaru Park — part of the former Imperial Palace's outer precinct — lies just across Uchibori-dori, and Yasukuni Shrine's south approach runs nearby. This historical backdrop means tenants in the office floors look toward the palace moat and surrounding greenery, a setting rare in any major business district.

The government agencies, law firms, and consultancies that occupy the office floors benefit both from the calm environment and the short walk to Kudanshita Station, which connects directly to the Toei Shinjuku, Toei Mita, and Tokyo Metro Tozai lines.

## Summary

Kitanomaru Square, completed in January 2006, stands 26 stories and 111.85 m tall with approximately 59,130 sqm of floor space. Developed on the former Nippon Credit Bank site, it blends retail, office, and residential use across its floors, all within easy reach of Kudanshita Station and the historic landscape of Chiyoda Ward.
