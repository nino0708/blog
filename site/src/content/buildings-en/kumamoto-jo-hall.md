## A Convention Hall in the Shadow of Kumamoto Castle

Kumamoto-jo Hall (KUMAMOTO-JO HALL) opened in September 2019 in the Sakuramachi district of Kumamoto City, within sight of Kumamoto Castle. The building was designed by Nikken Sekkei in collaboration with Taiko Sekkei, and built by a joint venture led by Taisei Corporation. Total floor area is approximately 30,000 m², making it the largest convention facility in Kumamoto.

## The Sakuramachi Redevelopment

The hall forms the centerpiece of the Sakuramachi Kumamoto redevelopment project, a large-scale mixed-use development in central Kumamoto. The complex, known commercially as SAKURAMACHI Kumamoto, includes retail shops, a cinema complex, a hotel, and condominiums alongside the convention hall. On the building's rooftop, a garden terrace called the "Sakuramachi Garden" offers views of Kumamoto Castle's keep at approximately 30 metres above street level — connecting the new structure directly to the historic site it stands beside.

The project's origins predate the April 2016 Kumamoto earthquakes, but the disaster gave the redevelopment added weight. The earthquakes inflicted severe damage on Kumamoto Castle — collapsing walls, towers, and stone foundations across large sections of the complex — and left a lasting mark on the city center. Opening a major new convention complex in its shadow became a marker of Kumamoto's recovery, and the hall was widely seen in that light when it opened.

## Anchoring a Recovering City

The south-facing Hanabatake Plaza connects the hall with the tram-lined city center, threading visitors and residents through the same public space. By attracting national and international meetings and conferences to Kumamoto, the facility supports economic recovery alongside cultural life.

Restoration of Kumamoto Castle continues alongside the ongoing activity in Sakuramachi. The pairing of a modern convention center with the centuries-old fortification makes this corner of central Kumamoto an unusual combination of old and new, anchored by the same cityscape and the same civic purpose.

## Summary

Completed in September 2019, Kumamoto-jo Hall spans approximately 30,000 m² in the Sakuramachi district of central Kumamoto. Designed by Nikken Sekkei as part of a major mixed-use redevelopment, it now serves as the city's principal convention venue — and a reminder of Kumamoto's resilience since the 2016 earthquakes. A visit is best paired with a walk to Kumamoto Castle, whose keep is visible from the rooftop garden.
