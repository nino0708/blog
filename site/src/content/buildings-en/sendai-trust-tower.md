## The Tallest Tower in Tōhoku

Sendai Trust Tower was completed in 2010 in the Ichibancho district of Aoba Ward, Sendai. Rising 180 metres over 37 floors, it was the tallest structure in the Tōhoku and northern Japan regions at the time of its opening. The project was developed by Mori Trust and designed by Matsuda Hirata Sekkei.

The Ichibancho district sits within easy walking distance of JR Sendai Station. What had been a block of smaller buildings was consolidated through redevelopment into a single high-rise: a retail base, office floors in the middle, and a full-service international hotel occupying the upper levels. The stacked programme means the tower serves both the daily business commuter and the traveller arriving for Sendai's festivals and regional tourism.

## A New Benchmark for the Sendai Skyline

Before this tower opened, no building in Tōhoku had surpassed the 180-metre mark. That milestone carried more than symbolic weight: it signalled Sendai's ambition to present itself as a genuine regional metropolis, complete with modern office stock and international hospitality. The floors fitted to international office standards attracted corporate tenants that in turn supported the surrounding Ichibancho retail corridor.

The surrounding arcade streets — including the covered shopping street of Ichibancho — wind past the tower's base, and on clear days the upper floors command views of the Kitakami Mountains and the Zaō range.

## Summary

Completed in 2010 at 37 storeys and 180 metres, Sendai Trust Tower remains the defining high-rise of the Tōhoku region, combining hotel, office and retail in a single tower that anchors Sendai's central business district. If you are planning a stay in Sendai, hotels in the Ichibancho area put you within easy reach of this landmark and the city's famous covered arcades.
