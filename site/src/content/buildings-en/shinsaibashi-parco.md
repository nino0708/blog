## A New Face for Shinsaibashi-suji

Walk south along Shinsaibashi-suji, Osaka's oldest covered shopping arcade, and a 14-story tower comes into view on the east side of the street. Shinsaibashi PARCO opened in November 2020 with a total floor area of approximately 58,000 square meters across 14 above-ground floors and 2 basement levels. Takenaka Corporation designed and built the entire complex, a scale of involvement that gave the project an unusual degree of architectural coherence. Fashion, food, art installations, and entertainment venues are stacked floor by floor, each level offering a different atmosphere.

The site carries significant commercial history. The Sogo Shinsaibashi store, one of the largest department stores in Osaka's Minami district, operated here for decades before Sogo's bankruptcy in 2000 ended its run. The building that PARCO now occupies replaced the former Sogo structure after years of planning and negotiation over how best to use such a prominent location in Osaka's most-visited shopping district.

## Shinsaibashi's Retail Renaissance

The late 2010s brought a cascade of changes to Shinsaibashi. The Daimaru Shinsaibashi store's main building, rebuilt and reopened in 2019 immediately adjacent to the PARCO site, kicked off a streak of large-scale openings that transformed the northern end of Shinsaibashi-suji. PARCO's arrival in 2020 added a second major anchor, creating a concentration of large commercial facilities in a stretch of street that inbound tourists and local shoppers alike treat as a primary destination.

International visitor numbers to Osaka had been climbing steeply before 2020, and the city's retail developers bet that an upgraded Shinsaibashi could capture a growing share of that traffic. The opening timing, coinciding with the early phase of COVID-19 disruptions, was unlucky, but Shinsaibashi PARCO has since established itself as a fixture of Minami's commercial geography.

## Summary

Shinsaibashi PARCO opened in November 2020 as a 14-story, 58,000 m² retail complex on the site of the former Sogo Shinsaibashi store in Chuo Ward, Osaka. Designed and built by Takenaka Corporation, it marks a new chapter for one of Japan's most famous shopping streets. Start your Minami walk at PARCO, follow Shinsaibashi-suji south toward Namba, and you'll cover the full arc of Osaka's retail reinvention.
