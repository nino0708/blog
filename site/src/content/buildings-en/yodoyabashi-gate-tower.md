## A New Tower for the Financial District

Yodoyabashi Gate Tower was completed in 2025 and opened in July 2026, rising 29 storeys and 135 metres above the Kitahama district of Osaka's Chuo Ward. The complex has a total floor area of approximately 132,330 square metres, with retail space on the basement and lower floors and offices occupying the upper storeys. An underground connection links directly to Yodoyabashi Station, served by both the Osaka Metro Midosuji Line and the Keihan Main Line — one of the best-connected stations in the city centre.

The Kitahama and Yodoyabashi area has long been Osaka's financial core: banks, securities firms, and insurance companies maintain head offices along streets that follow the old rice-trading canal network. New towers in this district do not arrive frequently, and the Gate Tower's 135-metre profile adds a visible new accent to a skyline shaped largely by earlier generations of office buildings.

## Osaka's Continuing Urban Renewal

Yodoyabashi Gate Tower's opening in 2026 places it alongside a broader wave of urban renewal transforming central Osaka. Across the city, [Grand Green Osaka](/en/buildings/grand-green-osaka) (opened 2024) on the former Umeda Freight Yard site and the redevelopment of the Nakanoshima corridor represent a generation of projects reshaping the city's core. The Gate Tower contributes to this picture from the older, more compact Kitahama side.

The direct station connection keeps commuters dry in Osaka's rainy seasons and positions the tower well for attracting tenants from the financial sector already embedded in this neighbourhood.
