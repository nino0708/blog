## A New Symbol Rising over Sannomiya

Standing just north of the Sannomiya intersection at the heart of Kobe, the Kobe Sannomiya Hankyu Building is a 29-story mixed-use tower completed in 2021. With a height of 121 meters, it was developed by Hankyu Hanshin Properties directly above Hankyu Sannomiya Station, replacing the original Sannomiya Hankyu Building that had served as Kobe's main gateway since 1936.

The decision to rebuild was driven by aging infrastructure and seismic safety concerns. Construction proceeded while Hankyu's Kobe Main Line remained in full operation, a logistical feat that made the project a symbolic first step in the broader Sannomiya redevelopment initiative. The lower floors are home to the EKIZO Kobe Sannomiya retail complex, while hotel rooms and commercial spaces occupy the upper floors.

A seamless connection to the station concourse means passengers can move from the platform to shops without stepping outside, making the building convenient for both commuters and visitors. The glass curtain-wall facade, accented by strong vertical lines, engages in a visual dialogue with the surrounding urban fabric while asserting a clear vertical identity. After sunset, the illuminated retail floors cast a warm glow over the busy intersection below.

## The Starting Point of an Urban Chain Reaction

Kobe City has branded the area around the three converging rail lines — JR, Hankyu, and Hanshin — as "Sannomiya Cross Sea," a large-scale urban regeneration zone. The Kobe Sannomiya Hankyu Building stands as the first major completed project within this framework. Visible from across Kobe Harbor, its 121-meter silhouette joins Port Tower and the Maritime Museum to form a renewed skyline for the port city.

In the years since opening, related redevelopment projects have continued to materialize nearby, including the reconstruction of the Kobe City Hall No. 1 building. With a total floor area of 28,850 square meters, the tower is the anchor and symbol of an ongoing transformation that is steadily reshaping central Sannomiya.

## Summary

The Kobe Sannomiya Hankyu Building, completed in 2021 with 29 floors above ground, a height of 121 meters, and a total floor area of 28,850 square meters, has redefined Kobe's main gateway. As the first completed landmark of the Sannomiya Cross Sea redevelopment plan, it anchors a chain of urban renewal that continues to unfold. If you find yourself passing through Sannomiya, look up before you reach the ticket gate — the vertical lines stretching skyward tell the story of a city in motion.
