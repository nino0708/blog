## High-Rise Living on the Edge of Nakanoshima

In 2025, a 46-storey residential tower was completed in Fukushima 2-chome, Fukushima Ward, Osaka. The building, Cielia Tower Nakanoshima, was developed by Kanden Fudosan Kaihatsu — the property arm of the Kansai Electric Power Group — and stands at the western approach to the Nakanoshima district. Nakanoshima is a long, narrow island formed between the Dojima River and the Tosabori River, known as the civic and cultural core of central Osaka: city hall, the Central Public Hall and the Museum of Art are all concentrated there, alongside major office towers. The arrival of a 46-storey residential tower on the district's edge signals a broadening of its character.

Fukushima Ward has been gaining residential popularity in recent years, driven by good access to the city centre and a relative softness in land prices compared to Umeda and Nakanoshima proper. The site sits on a main arterial road surrounded by a mix of low-rise housing and commercial buildings — typical of the ward's transitional urban texture — making the tower's scale all the more conspicuous.

## A New Residential Presence on the Osaka Skyline

Kanden Fudosan Kaihatsu has developed residential projects across the Kansai region under the Cielia brand. This tower is among the largest in the portfolio. At 46 storeys, the building adds a prominent new element to the skyline visible from central Nakanoshima looking west, and from the elevated sections of the Hanshin Expressway. The upper floors are said to command views extending to Osaka Bay to the west and the Yodo River corridor to the north.

## Summary

Cielia Tower Nakanoshima was completed in 2025 as a 46-storey residential tower in Fukushima 2-chome, Fukushima Ward, developed by Kanden Fudosan Kaihatsu. It represents the ongoing expansion of high-rise residential development into a part of Osaka previously dominated by offices and public institutions. Nearby, the twin [Nakanoshima Festival Tower](/buildings/nakanoshima-festival-tower) and [Nakanoshima Festival Tower West](/buildings/nakanoshima-festival-tower-west) offer a different register — commercial towers with cultural facilities at their base — making the Nakanoshima waterfront an interesting walk for anyone curious about the variety of tall buildings now shaping this part of the city.
