## A New Landmark East of the Scramble

Walk east from Shibuya's famous scramble crossing for about three minutes and a sleek tower comes into view. SHIBUYA AXSH opened in 2024, rising 23 floors and 120 metres above the Shibuya 2-chome district. The name "AXSH" evokes the idea of intersecting axes — fitting for a building conceived as a hub where people, ideas, and information converge at Shibuya's storied crossroads.

The project was driven by the Shibuya 2-chome 17 District Urban Redevelopment Association, with Tokyu Group as a key participant. Design was handled by a joint venture of Tokyu Design Consultants, Mitsubishi Jisho Sekkei, and Pacific Consultants; Takenaka Corporation carried out the construction. Upper floors are dedicated to offices, while the lower levels gather retail and cultural tenants. The building's proximity to Shibuya Station makes it a compelling address for start-ups and established companies alike.

## Shibuya's East Side Transformation

For much of the 2010s, Shibuya's redevelopment energy focused on the western and southern flanks of the station — Hikarie in 2012, Scramble Square in 2019. SHIBUYA AXSH belongs to a newer wave reshaping the quieter eastern approach. The site previously held a mix of small office buildings and commercial properties; the urban redevelopment scheme consolidated those rights into a single project, freeing up an open plaza that links the tower to the pedestrian flow from the station.

The result is a building that feels integrated into the neighbourhood rather than imposed upon it. Nearby Shibuya Stream and Scramble Square complete an arc of renewed streets, and the growing walkability of the whole area makes it easy to take in several landmarks in a single stroll.

## Summary

SHIBUYA AXSH was completed in 2024 — 23 floors, 120 metres tall. Developed by the Shibuya 2-chome 17 District Urban Redevelopment Association and designed by a joint venture led by Tokyu Design Consultants, it marks the latest chapter in Shibuya's long-running reinvention. Head east from the station and let the tower guide you into one of Tokyo's most exciting urban corridors.
