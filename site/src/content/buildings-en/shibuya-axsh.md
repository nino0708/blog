## A New Face for East Shibuya

Completed in 2024, Shibuya Axsh rises 23 stories and approximately 120 meters above the Miyamasuzaka neighborhood on Shibuya Station's east exit side. The building was developed as part of the Shibuya 2-chome 17 District urban redevelopment project, standing adjacent to Shibuya Stream along the Shibuya River. With a total floor area of around 44,500 square meters spread across its floors above and below ground, it represents the latest chapter in Shibuya's decade-long transformation.

The name "Axsh" derives from "Axis of Shibuya," reflecting the building's intended role as a new north–south exchange corridor for the district.

## Anchoring the East Side of a Reborn District

While much of Shibuya's redevelopment attention fell on the station's west and central areas—Scramble Square, Hikarie—Axsh anchors the quieter east side. The lower floors accommodate halls, meeting rooms, and retail spaces, while the upper floors hold flexible office areas serving a range of tenant sizes.

The design was handled by a joint venture of Tokyu Design Consultant, Mitsubishi Jisho Sekkei, and Pacific Consultants, with Takenaka Corporation as general contractor. Its completion draws the Miyamasuzaka corridor fully into Shibuya's renewed urban fabric.

## Summary

Shibuya Axsh, completed in 2024 at 23 stories and roughly 120 meters tall, marks the eastward completion of Shibuya's long redevelopment arc. Standing beside Shibuya Stream along the river that once ran underground through this neighborhood, the tower is both a practical office and commercial anchor and a visual landmark for east Shibuya. Walk the Shibuya River promenade to see the building's riverside facade at its best.
