## A Waterfront Landmark on the Shinano River Estuary

At 6-1 Bandaijima, Chuo Ward, Niigata City, the Toki Messe Bandaijima Building rises 31 floors and 140.5 meters above the Bandaijima waterfront. Completed in 2003 and designed by Nikken Sekkei, the building is the centerpiece of a mixed-use complex that also includes the Toki Messe convention hall and the adjacent 100-meter Rainbow Tower observation structure. Together they form the most recognizable skyline element along Niigata's harbor.

The tower's lower and mid floors accommodate hotel and office tenants connected by covered walkways to the large exhibition halls and conference rooms of the Toki Messe convention facility. With its 31-story profile rising against the Sea of Japan horizon and the wide mouth of the Shinano River, the complex serves as Niigata's principal venue for national and international conferences as well as an anchor for the city's tourism and hospitality sector.

## Bandaijima and Niigata's Urban Waterfront Vision

The Bandaijima district occupies land adjacent to Niigata Port, which served as one of the major gateways on the Japan Sea coast for centuries. As port operations modernized, the city identified an opportunity to transform the harbor edge into an urban amenity. Planning for the Bandaijima development project took shape in the 1980s, culminating in the opening of Toki Messe and the Bandaijima Building in 2003.

At 140.5 meters, the tower is one of the tallest buildings in Niigata City — a height that reflects the scale of ambition behind the project. Seen from across the Shinano River or from the old city center, the Bandaijima Building and Rainbow Tower together mark where Niigata's urban core meets the waterfront, a pairing that has become inseparable from the city's modern identity.

## Summary

The Toki Messe Bandaijima Building was completed in 2003, reaching 31 floors and 140.5 meters above Niigata's Bandaijima waterfront. Designed by Nikken Sekkei, the tower combines convention, hotel, and office functions in a complex that also includes the Toki Messe exhibition hall and the Rainbow Tower. For visitors to Niigata, a walk along the riverside promenade from the old city toward Bandaijima — ending with a look up at these two towers above the Shinano estuary — shows directly how thoroughly the waterfront has been transformed since the port era.
