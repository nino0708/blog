## A Landmark Reborn Beside Hisaya Odori Park

Facing the green expanse of Hisaya Odori Park at the heart of Nagoya's Sakae district, the Chunichi Building completed in July 2023 as a 33-story, 158-meter tower that replaced its predecessor on the same site. With a gross floor area of 117,293 square meters across 33 floors above ground and 5 below, the building was entirely designed and built by Takenaka Corporation for developer Chubu Nippon Building. Its steel, reinforced concrete, and steel-reinforced concrete hybrid structure allowed the deep five-basement podium required in a dense urban center. After full opening on April 23, 2024, the complex added a significant new address to Sakae's office and hospitality landscape.

The building's program encompasses office floors, a performance hall, retail, dining, and The Royal Park Hotel Iconic Nagoya, ensuring that the cultural legacy of the original Chunichi Building — a Nagoya institution since 1966 — carries forward in modern form.

## Sakae's Heritage Redeveloped

The original Chunichi Building opened in 1966 and became central to Nagoya's cultural life, hosting theater performances and exhibitions for decades. When demolition finished at the end of 2020, the redevelopment focused on retaining that civic purpose while dramatically expanding usable floor area and updating the building's environmental performance.

The completed tower's facade responds to the park setting: its lines run vertically to emphasize height while horizontal elements connect visually to the tree canopy of Hisaya Odori below. Standing on the park promenade and looking toward the building, 33 floors of glass and steel rise from a low podium level that blends with the streetscape — a composition that respects both the scale of the park and the demands of contemporary urban development.

## Summary

The Chunichi Building was completed in July 2023 with 33 floors above ground, 5 below, standing 158 m tall with a total floor area of 117,293 m². Takenaka Corporation handled design, supervision, and construction for Chubu Nippon Building, delivering a mixed-use tower that carries on sixty years of civic history in Nagoya's Sakae district. Walk along Hisaya Odori Park and let the view of the tower rising above the trees mark the spot where old Nagoya and new Nagoya meet.
