## A Waterfront Arena Built for the Olympics

On the reclaimed land of Ariake, a district in Tokyo's Koto City that has become one of the capital's principal venues for large-scale sport and exhibitions, Ariake Arena was completed in 2019. Built specifically to host volleyball competitions at the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games — held in 2021 following the pandemic postponement — the arena holds approximately 15,000 spectators in its main hall and occupies five floors to a height of 37 metres.

The building's roof is its most visible feature: a broad, curved canopy that sweeps over the structure in a form designed to read as open and welcoming from the surrounding waterfront plazas. The interior follows the logic of a multipurpose venue: a large main arena with flexible seating configurations, a sub-arena, meeting rooms and athlete facilities that can be rearranged to accommodate events of different types and scales.

## After the Games

The Tokyo 2020 Games produced several permanent venues around Ariake — the Ariake Gymnastics Centre, the Ariake Urban Sports Park, and the arena itself — with the intention that each would serve a legacy function once the games were finished. Ariake Arena transitioned to commercial operation after the Games, hosting major concerts, professional basketball and volleyball matches, and combat sports events. Its informal nickname, "Ariko" (ありコー), reflects the degree to which it has been absorbed into the everyday vocabulary of Tokyo sports and entertainment.

The arena is reachable on foot from Ariake-Tennis-no-Mori Station on the Yurikamome line, which connects Ariake to Odaiba and Shinbashi. The surrounding neighbourhood — Ariake Garden (a hotel and shopping complex opened in 2020), the Tokyo Big Sight exhibition centre, and Palette Town's successor developments — makes the area a self-contained destination for a day out on Tokyo Bay.

## Summary

Ariake Arena, completed in 2019, is a 15,000-seat indoor arena in Ariake, Koto City, Tokyo. Built by Tokyo Metropolitan Government as the volleyball venue for the Tokyo 2020 Olympic and Paralympic Games, it stands five floors and 37 metres tall. Since the Games it has operated as a major venue for concerts, sports and other events, and has become a key component of the Ariake waterfront's post-Olympic legacy.
