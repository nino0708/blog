## Twin Towers by the Castle

In the Osaka Business Park (OBP) district of Chuo Ward — immediately southeast of Osaka Castle Park — two near-identical office towers stand in symmetrical alignment. TWIN21, completed in 1986, comprises the MID Tower and the Panasonic Tower (formerly National Tower), each rising 38 stories and designed by Nikken Sekkei. Their paired silhouettes, framed against the green of the castle grounds, have defined the eastern edge of Osaka's skyline since the late 1980s.

The towers are connected at the base by a covered gallery-style arcade, providing weather-protected pedestrian movement between them. Lower floors contain retail and dining tenants; upper floors are entirely given over to offices. The address — 2-1-61 Shiromi, Chuo Ward — places them at the heart of the OBP grid, which was master-planned as a concentrated business district on the site of a former gas works.

## Anchoring the Osaka Business Park

The OBP redevelopment was an Osaka City-led project of the 1980s and 1990s that brought several major high-rise towers to the former gas-works site. TWIN21 was among the first to be completed, and its scale and dual-tower layout set the visual reference point for everything built around it — including the Crystal Tower (1990) and the WTC Cosmo Tower (1995) that followed in Osaka's ongoing high-rise expansion.

From Osaka Castle's tenshu (main keep), both towers appear in the middle-ground view southeast, their reflective curtain walls catching the light while the castle's stone foundations anchor the foreground. The pairing of feudal and modern Osaka in a single viewfinder has made this a widely photographed angle on the city.

## Summary

TWIN21 (1986, 38 floors, 157 m) consists of twin office towers in the Osaka Business Park, designed by Nikken Sekkei. The symmetrically positioned pair stands beside Osaka Castle Park and remains one of the most recognizable skyline elements in central Osaka.
