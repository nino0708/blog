## Fukuoka's Tenjin Big Bang Gets Its First Tower

Tenjin Business Center stands at 1-10-20 Tenjin, Chuo Ward, Fukuoka. Completed in September 2021, the building rises 19 stories with 2 basement floors to a height of approximately 89 meters, with a total floor area of about 61,100 square meters. It was developed by Fukuoka Jisho, one of Fukuoka's leading real estate companies, and built primarily in steel-frame construction.

The Tenjin Big Bang is an urban redevelopment program that Fukuoka City launched in 2015 to revitalize the aging commercial core of the Tenjin district. The initiative combined relaxed height restrictions — aviation-law ceilings had previously kept buildings low-rise — with floor-area-ratio bonuses to encourage large-scale demolition and reconstruction. Before the program, much of central Tenjin was occupied by postwar buildings that had aged beyond their economically useful life. Tenjin Business Center was among the very first projects to be completed under this framework, serving as an early proof of concept for the city's ambitious plan.

## Fukuoka Jisho and the Central City

Fukuoka Jisho is a major developer based in Fukuoka with a long track record of commercial, retail, and office projects across the city. By securing a prime site at the very heart of Tenjin for this project, the company positioned itself as a leading force in the district's renewal. The building offers a standard floor plate of approximately 2,370 square meters (around 717 tsubo), making it suitable for large corporate tenants seeking headquarters or major branch-office space in western Japan.

The location at Tenjin 1-chome is surrounded by multiple transit options: Fukuoka City Subway Tenjin Station and Nishitetsu Fukuoka (Tenjin) Station are both immediately adjacent, with subway Tenjin-Minami Station also nearby. This combination of rail access from several different lines places the building at the most connected address in Kyushu.

## Summary

Tenjin Business Center is a 19-story, approximately 89-meter office tower completed in September 2021 in the Tenjin district of Chuo Ward, Fukuoka. Developed by Fukuoka Jisho under the city's Tenjin Big Bang initiative, it stands as a tangible early result of a policy designed to transform an aging commercial center into a competitive modern business district. Exploring Tenjin today — watching new towers rise alongside covered shopping arcades that have been there for decades — offers a vivid window into how Japanese cities renew themselves.
