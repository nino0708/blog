## A Waterfront Office Tower in the Shinagawa Seaside District

Shinagawa Seaside East Tower completed in August 2004 in Higashishinagawa, Shinagawa Ward. The building stands 23 stories above ground with one basement level, reaching 100.41 m and providing approximately 44,070 sqm of total floor area. Kajima Corporation handled both design and construction in a single-contractor arrangement.

The building occupies the Shinagawa Seaside development zone, a former industrial waterfront that was comprehensively redeveloped from the late 1990s. Together with other towers in the Shinagawa Seaside Forest complex nearby, East Tower helped define this part of the Tokyo waterfront as a destination for technology companies seeking large floor plates in a relatively open urban setting.

## Waterfront Character and Transport Links

The East Tower's setting differs markedly from the dense high-rise corridors of central Tokyo. The Rinkai Line's Shinagawa Seaside Station is within easy walking distance, offering direct connections to Osaki and onward to the Yamanote Line, and to Shin-Kiba and the Keiyo Line corridor. That connectivity, combined with generous public open space at street level, has made the Shinagawa Seaside area an alternative office location for companies that prefer campus-style surroundings.

At 100.41 m, the tower does not dominate the skyline, but its height stands out in a low-rise waterfront landscape and gives upper-floor offices views toward the waterway and surrounding development.

## Summary

Shinagawa Seaside East Tower, completed in August 2004, stands 23 stories and 100.41 m tall with approximately 44,070 sqm of floor space. Designed and built by Kajima Corporation, it is part of the Shinagawa Seaside waterfront redevelopment in Shinagawa Ward and represents an early chapter in the transformation of Tokyo's industrial waterfront into an office district.
