## A Tower in Roppongi's Business Quarter

Roppongi T-Cube rises 27 floors at 3-1-1 Roppongi, Minato Ward, overlooking the Roppongi-nichome intersection where National Route 415 meets Roppongi-dori. The building completed in 2003 and is served by eight elevators. Each standard floor provides more than 500 tsubo — roughly 1,650 square metres — of open office space, a scale that suits tenants who need large, undivided floorplates.

Roppongi underwent a substantial transformation in the early 2000s. Roppongi Hills, the large mixed-use complex by Mori Building, opened the same year as T-Cube. Tokyo Midtown followed in 2007. What had been a neighbourhood primarily known for nightlife gradually accumulated a dense cluster of international businesses: law firms, consulting practices, financial institutions, and foreign-capital companies drawn by the high-specification offices, the proximity to embassies in nearby Minato Ward, and the direct transport connections.

T-Cube sits a short distance from Ark Mori Building (1986), one of the earlier towers that established Roppongi-itchome as an office address. Together these buildings mark the layered development of Roppongi over three decades, each generation adding capacity to a neighbourhood that positions itself as Tokyo's most internationally oriented business district.
