## A Green-Framed Office Complex in Bunkyo

Bunkyo Green Court stands at Hongo-Komagome 2-28-8 in Bunkyo, Tokyo, directly linked to Hongo-Komagome Station on the Tokyo Metro Namboku Line. Bunkyo is Tokyo's academic heartland — home to the University of Tokyo's Hongo Campus, Koishikawa Korakuen garden, and a cluster of publishing houses and medical institutions — and Hongo-Komagome sits at its northern edge, where residential streets blend quietly with the outer fringes of central Tokyo.

The Namboku Line opened its first section in 1996 and extended to Meguro in 2000, making Hongo-Komagome Station a relatively new node in the city's rail network. Bunkyo Green Court was developed in the late 1990s to take advantage of the station's opening, positioning itself as a subway-connected rental office and retail building in a neighbourhood with stable demand from academia, publishing, and healthcare tenants.

## Office Space with Green Surroundings

The "Green Court" name reflects the landscaped approach and greenery around the building — a distinction in a part of Tokyo that has remained less intensively developed than the ward's southern core around Hongo-sanchome. The design incorporates natural materials in the lower floors and entrance to blend with Bunkyo's subdued, studious character. Tenants benefit from direct Namboku Line access to Ichigaya, Yotsuya, Tameike-Sanno, and Meguro without transfers — a straight run through the centre and south of the city.

## Summary

Bunkyo Green Court at Hongo-Komagome 2-28-8 is an office and retail complex directly connected to Hongo-Komagome Station on the Tokyo Metro Namboku Line, completed in the late 1990s. It sits in one of Tokyo's quieter districts — Bunkyo, known for its universities, publishers, and medical institutions — and draws its identity from the green landscape around it and from the convenient subway access the Namboku Line provides to central and southern Tokyo.
