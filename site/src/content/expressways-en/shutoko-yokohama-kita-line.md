## Through the City, Under the City

The Metropolitan Expressway Kanagawa Route 7 — called the Yokohama Kita Line — opened on March 18, 2017, connecting Namamugi Junction in Tsurumi Ward to Yokohama Kohoku Junction in Kohoku Ward over a distance of 8.2 kilometers. The great majority of that distance, roughly 5.9 kilometers, passes through the Yokohama Kita Tunnel, bored beneath the densely built suburbs of northern Yokohama. Running underground kept noise and visual intrusion to a minimum in established residential areas that otherwise would have been bisected by an elevated highway.

The full route is four lanes (two in each direction) with a 60 km/h speed limit. Shield tunneling was used for most of the underground section, with tunnel diameters of approximately 13 meters to accommodate the required traffic lanes and emergency equipment.

## Part of a Larger Network

The Yokohama Kita Line was designed from the outset as one segment of a wider orbital network. On March 22, 2020, the Yokohama Kita-Nishi Line (a 7.1-kilometer extension) opened, connecting Yokohama Kohoku Junction to Yokohama Aoba Junction and thereby creating a continuous link between the Tomei Expressway at the Yokohama-Aoba interchange and the port areas of central Yokohama. Together, the two sections allow drivers to travel from the Tomei Expressway to the Yokohama waterfront without passing through any urban surface roads.

## Summary

The Metropolitan Expressway Yokohama Kita Line opened in March 2017, running 8.2 kilometers — most of it underground — between Namamugi Junction and Yokohama Kohoku Junction. Extended by the Yokohama Kita-Nishi Line in 2020, it now forms an important arc in Yokohama's expressway ring, quietly channeling traffic through tunnels that most surface travelers never see.
